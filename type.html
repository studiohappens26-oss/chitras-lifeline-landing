Rounded directions

Ordered from most conventional to softest. Roundness is a spectrum: some faces are round in their shapes (circular o and e), others are round at their stroke ends (literal rounded caps). The second kind reads warmer. 08 is the one I would actually ship.

06 Rounded Geometric Poppins (single family)

Perfect circles. Poppins is built on a geometric skeleton — the o, e and a are near-true circles, which is the most legible kind of roundness. It is friendly and unpretentious, and it renders reliably on every Android phone in the country. Risk: it is the single most-used font in Indian digital design. Your page will look competent, but you will see the same face on a hundred other clinic sites.
