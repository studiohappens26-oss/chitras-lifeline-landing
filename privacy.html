Privacy notice

How the clinic uses the details you send through this website. Last updated November 2026.

In short

We use your details only to reply to you.

Who we are

Chitra's Lifeline Clinic, Ward No. 4, LMN Arcade, 17-B/4, 1169, Opposite Rail Wheel Factory, Yelahanka New Town, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560064 ("the clinic", "we"). The clinic decides how the details collected on this website are used.

What we collect

When you use the booking form we receive the details you type in: your name, mobile number, the treatment you're interested in, the best time to call, and any note you add. We also record which page you sent it from. We don't ask for medical records through this website, so please don't send them here.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your enquiry and to arrange a consultation. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not sell it or share it for marketing.

How it reaches us

  • The form opens WhatsApp on your phone with your details written out. Nothing is sent until you press send. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.
  • A copy of the form is also sent to the clinic's records so we can call you back if the WhatsApp message doesn't go through. This copy is processed by our website host and the services the clinic uses to receive it.

Consent

We only process these details because you tick the consent box on the form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us, and we will stop contacting you about the enquiry.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, and then deleted. If you become a patient, your clinical records are kept as the law requires for medical records, separately from this website.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us for a summary of the details we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete them, and nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. To do any of this, or to raise a concern, call +91 81975 16940 or message us on WhatsApp. If you aren't satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies of its own. If the clinic adds advertising or analytics measurement, this notice will be updated to say so before it is switched on.

Embedded content

The map and the Instagram videos on the home page are provided by Google and Instagram. They load only when you scroll to them, and those companies' own privacy policies apply to them.
