02 — Laser Hair Removal

Indian skin needs different settings.

Most Indian skin sits at Fitzpatrick IV–V, which holds far more melanin than the skin these machines are preset for. Push the wrong energy through it and you get burns or fresh pigmentation. Under-dose it and the hair simply comes back. Reading your skin type correctly is the entire job — and it's a clinical judgement, not a dial setting.

Questions

The things people actually ask

Does botox hurt?

The needle is very fine, and most people describe it as a brief pinch per injection point. Numbing cream is applied first if you'd prefer. The whole appointment takes about 15–20 minutes.

Will my face look frozen?

That happens when a standard dose is applied without reading the individual face. The goal here is the opposite — the lines soften, and you keep the full range of your expression. Dosing is planned around your muscle strength and how you actually move your face.

How long do botox results last?

Typically three to six months. It varies with metabolism, muscle strength, dose and how long you've been having treatment — long-term patients often find the interval stretches out.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It gives long-term reduction rather than permanent removal. Most people need six to eight sessions spaced four to six weeks apart, after which hair is far finer and sparser. Occasional maintenance sessions keep it that way. Hormonal conditions such as PCOS can change the picture, which is why it's assessed first.

Is laser safe on Indian skin?

Yes, with the right device and the right settings. Deeper skin tones carry more melanin, so energy levels, pulse duration and cooling all have to be adjusted — otherwise you risk burns or post-inflammatory pigmentation. This is exactly why it matters that a dermatologist sets the parameters.

I have a wedding coming up. When should I start?

Work backwards from the date. Botox is best done three to four weeks ahead so it has fully settled. Laser hair removal needs a run of six-plus months to complete a course. Medi-facials are usually timed five to seven days before the event, with a short course in the months leading up to it.

Do you treat men?

Yes. Botox, laser hair removal (beard shaping, back and chest), acne treatment, hair-fall management and PRP are all commonly done for male patients here.
