Ask a question
Does an anti-wrinkle injection hurt?

The needle is very fine, and most people describe a brief pinch at each injection point. Numbing cream is applied first if you'd prefer. The whole appointment takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

Will my face look frozen?

That tends to happen when a standard dose is used without reading the individual face. Here the dose is planned around your muscle strength and how you actually move your face, so the aim is softer lines with your expressions intact.

How long do anti-wrinkle results last?

Typically three to six months. It varies with metabolism, muscle strength, dose and how long you've been having treatment.

Is laser hair reduction permanent?

It gives long-term reduction rather than permanent removal. Most people need six to eight sessions spaced four to six weeks apart, after which hair is finer and sparser, with occasional maintenance sessions. Hormonal conditions such as PCOS can change the picture, which is why it's assessed first.

Is laser safe on Indian skin?

With the right device and the right settings. Deeper skin tones carry more melanin, so energy, pulse duration and cooling all have to be adjusted, or there is a risk of burns or post-inflammatory pigmentation. That's why the doctor sets the parameters.

I have a wedding coming up. When should I start?

Work backwards from the date. Anti-wrinkle injections are best done three to four weeks ahead so they have fully settled. Laser hair reduction needs six months or more to complete a course. Medi-facials are usually timed five to seven days before the event, with a short course in the months leading up to it.

Do you treat men?

Yes. Anti-wrinkle injections, laser hair reduction (beard shaping, back and chest), acne treatment, hair-fall management and PRP are all commonly done for men here.
